🎨 Student Task 2: CSS Styles and Flexbox Cards

Goal: Connect your HTML page with an external CSS file (styles.css) and explore key design properties like colors, margins, padding, box-shadows, and Flexbox layouts.

Your Exercises:

  1. Open styles.css and change the main background color of the body.
  2. Adjust the padding and margin of the card class. What happens if you make padding extremely large or margin extremely small?
  3. Change the font-family of the headings from Arial, sans-serif to a serif style like Georgia, serif or 'Times New Roman', serif.
  4. Customize the travel cards by modifying the pricing tag style (.price-tag) with your own background color.
  5. Challenge: Add a hover effect in styles.css to make the travel cards scale larger and cast a bigger shadow when you mouse over them!
